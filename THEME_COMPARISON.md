# Nexus Theme vs Astra vs Hello Elementor - Feature Comparison

## Executive Summary

**Nexus** is a **purpose-built B2B technical theme** specifically designed for:
- Electrical & Control Systems companies
- AI/ML solution providers
- Embedded Systems manufacturers
- Technical product/service businesses

While **Astra** and **Hello Elementor** are excellent **general-purpose themes** for any website, **Nexus is specialized** with built-in features that would require multiple premium plugins on other themes.
