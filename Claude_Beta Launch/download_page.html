📦 Installation Steps

1

Close AutoCAD/BricsCAD

Make sure all instances of AutoCAD or BricsCAD are completely closed before proceeding.

2

Run the Installer

Double-click the downloaded UL_NEC_Checker_Installer.msi file and follow the installation wizard.

3

Enter Your License Key

When prompted, paste your license key from above. The installer will verify your license automatically.

4

Launch AutoCAD

Open AutoCAD or BricsCAD. The UL/NEC Checker toolbar should appear automatically. If not, go to View → Toolbars → UL/NEC Compliance Checker.

5

Run Your First Check

Open an electrical panel drawing and click "Run Compliance Check" to validate against 80+ UL508A/NEC rules!
